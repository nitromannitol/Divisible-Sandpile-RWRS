## What is claimed

The paper's registered statements are proved in Lean 4 from Mathlib and
the shared library `LatticeProb`, under the hypotheses displayed in their
frozen declarations, with no added axiom and no `sorry`.
Cited inputs are proposition-valued predicates in `RWRS/External/`,
carried as explicit hypotheses together with each theorem's own hypotheses.
Separate companion theorems discharge these predicates under the conditions
listed below; `CORRESPONDENCE.md` records the correspondence with the paper.
7 of the 9 cited inputs have registered proved companions.

## Reproducing it

```
elan toolchain install $(cat lean-toolchain)
lake exe cache get      # optional: prebuilt Mathlib
lake build RWRS
python3 tools/check_manifest.py     # frozen statements match their hashes
python3 tools/check_axioms.py       # no axiom closure contains sorryAx
python3 tools/check_warnings.py     # the build emits no unauthorized warning
python3 tools/check_constants.py    # parameter order for uniform constants
python3 tools/sync_docs.py --write  # generate manifest and input tables
python3 tools/check_coverage.py     # every paper statement is registered
python3 tools/certificate.py --check
```

`P_x(X_n = y) ≤ 2 sqrt(deg(y)/deg(x)) exp(-dist(x,y)^2/(2n))`,
for `n ≥ 1` on a connected, nontrivial, locally finite graph with measurable
singletons. Its sources are Carne (1985), Varopoulos (1985), and

radii `⌈N^(1/2+s)⌉`, `s > 0`, used in stabilization part (ii).
The constants may depend on the origin and on `s`.

## What is not claimed

- The cited theorems in `RWRS/External/` (the rows marked *assumed* above) are
  not proved here; each statement that uses one carries it as an explicit
  hypothesis. Companion proofs apply under the hypotheses listed above.
- The paper's open questions (Section 9) are questions; nothing there is
  formalized, and nothing here bears on them.
- The proof ideas and the related work of Sections 1.1 and 1.2 are
  outside the formalization.
